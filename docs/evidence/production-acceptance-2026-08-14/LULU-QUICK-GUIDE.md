# Lulu’s Studio quick guide

## Add and finish a garment

1. Open **Wardrobe** and tap **Add garment**.
2. Choose **Camera**, **Photos**, or **Describe**.
3. Check the garment facts, then save the draft.
4. Open the garment. Tap any image to view it full screen.
5. Under **Still needed**, tap **Magic** for the next missing photo.
6. Add a clear source photo of that exact view. For a back view, photograph the real back; Magic never invents unseen construction.
7. Review the result. Choose **Keep**, **Try again**, or **Cancel**.
8. Repeat until the garment is **Ready**.
9. Review the Shop preview, then choose **Make public** and **Publish**.

Camera and Photos always remain available beside Magic.

## Manage an order

1. Open **Orders** and choose the order that needs attention.
2. Check the transfer receipt.
3. Confirm payment only after the money arrives.
4. Check the garment, then mark it ready for pickup or delivery.
5. Mark it collected or delivered when the handoff is complete.
6. If a return is requested, review it before accepting it.
7. When the piece comes back, record receipt and send the refund.
8. Choose **Restock** if it can be sold again, or **Remove from sale** if it cannot.

The bottom action always opens the next useful step. Nothing becomes public, paid, delivered, refunded, or restocked until Lulu confirms it.
