# JUW catalogue atelier canon

**Status: LOCKED**

This file defines the permanent JustUrbanWears catalogue room. It is derived from the user-approved Garment 001 and Garment 002 `05/06/07` images, with the accepted Garment 003 packet serving as continuity validation.

The room already exists. It must not be reinvented.

## 1. Authority roles

### Primary room authority

The approved Garment 001 and Garment 002 catalogue images control:

- room geometry and layout
- prop placement and scale
- wall, floor and rug relationship
- camera family and negative space
- light direction, softness and colour temperature
- the restrained catalogue ambience

The accepted Garment 003 images validate that the same room survives another garment family.

### Icon authority only

`public/brand/icon.svg` and the approved logo-reveal or identity references control **only the geometry and coral colour of the standalone wall icon**.

They do not control:

- room architecture
- wall colour
- lighting
- signage scale
- prop selection
- placement of a wordmark
- creation of a branded reception wall

The full logo lockup and dark boutique/logo-wall mockups shown during review were supplied to correct the icon depiction. They are **not room proposals** and have zero atelier authority.

### Explicit non-authorities

The following may never redefine the room:

- garment source photographs or mannequin rooms
- campaign posters, hang tags or packaging mockups
- full `justurban wears / BY LULU` lockups
- dark reception-counter or showroom concepts
- generated room-lock boards
- rejected Garment 004 attempts

## 2. Fixed room composition

The permanent atelier is the light, warm JUW catalogue studio visible in the approved images:

- a clean warm cream/beige wall
- soft warm-neutral photographic light with gentle falloff
- natural skin rendering and restrained contrast
- no dark cinematic treatment and no orange cast
- one small standalone coral JUW icon plaque in the upper-left zone
- one tall cream ceramic vase at lower left
- one restrained arrangement of dry tan branches rising from that vase
- one slim brass garment rail at right
- sparse garments on wooden hangers in black, cream, beige/taupe and rust
- one rounded cream ottoman at lower right
- a neutral floor with the approved rug entering from the lower-right area
- generous uncluttered negative space around the model

The model occupies the same photographic family and scale across views. The room does not acquire new architecture when the pose changes.

## 3. Colour temperature and ambience

The approved ambience is:

- warm-neutral rather than dark amber
- softly illuminated rather than spotlighted
- calm catalogue photography rather than a dramatic luxury-showroom render
- low-clutter, natural and believable
- sufficiently warm to support the coral icon and rust garments without tinting Lulu's complexion orange

Visible cones of ceiling light, theatrical pools of light, heavy vignette, deep espresso walls or reception-lobby mood are failures.

### Photographic integration and camera realism

Lulu, the garment, footwear and room must read as one exposure in one physical space:

- light direction, colour temperature, softness and falloff agree across skin, cloth, brass, floor and walls
- footwear is grounded with plausible contact occlusion and shadow rather than floating or clipping
- subject edges remain naturally resolved with no cutout halo, pasted-room boundary or mismatched sharpness
- the camera stays level and catalogue-natural with believable focal-length perspective, preserved body scale and uniform image scaling
- skin and materials keep natural microtexture without noise overlays, over-sharpening, synthetic HDR, CGI sheen or beauty-filter smoothing

The room plate controls layout and light family, but realism is evaluated on the integrated photograph rather than by matching background pixels alone.

## 4. Wall icon lock

The wall contains one **standalone icon only**:

- two mirrored coral wardrobe panels
- a central female/hourglass negative-space silhouette
- two lower `L` forms

The icon is small, restrained and mounted in the approved upper-left position.

Forbidden on the wall:

- `justurban`
- `wears`
- `BY LULU`
- `JUW` or `JW`
- any full wordmark or lockup
- circles, triangles or substitute lower cutouts
- altered silhouette geometry
- enlarged reception-signage treatment

## 5. Forbidden room changes

Do not add or substitute:

- a reception counter
- mirror
- shelving wall or backroom display
- arched doorway
- vertical timber slats
- large indoor tree or second plant
- second vase
- visible ceiling fixtures
- additional signs or branded cards
- dark brown walls
- marble retail desk
- extra decorative architecture
- a larger or relocated logo

## 6. View continuity

- `05` changes nothing about the room; it is the front master.
- `06` changes the model to left profile only.
- `07` changes the model to right rear three-quarter only.

The room, icon, light, colour balance, prop count and composition remain within the same accepted set across all three views.

## 7. Acceptance gate

An output fails the atelier gate if it looks like a new interpretation of a warm boutique rather than the approved room.

An output fails the brand gate if the wall mark is anything other than the exact small standalone canonical icon.

When an accepted parent exists, do not regenerate the room to fix another layer.
