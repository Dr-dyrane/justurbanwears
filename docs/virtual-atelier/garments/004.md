## Construction lock

Garment 004 is:

- a long close black column dress
- approximately ankle/floor length according to the full reference
- minimal/sleeveless arm coverage beneath the contrast neckline
- a broad white folded/draped contrast band sitting low across the shoulders and upper chest
- an irregular natural fold rather than a perfectly symmetrical bridal cowl
- a narrow black body beneath the white fold
- subtle natural horizontal tension and creasing through waist and hip
- a straight, restrained lower silhouette and hem

Do not convert it into:

- a bridal gown
- a structured off-shoulder corset dress
- a flared or mermaid silhouette
- a loose sheath
- a dramatic couture cowl
- a strapless dress
- a dress with added sleeves

## Styling lock

- restrained gold jewelry
- clear/nude minimal heels

Styling references do not override Lulu's identity, body, atelier, or garment construction.

## Acceptance gates

### Face

Must read as real Lulu translated into JUW photography. No generic editorial substitute, narrowing, beautification, or complexion drift.

### Body

Dress must conform to the canon. Lulu may not shrink to fit the garment. Preserve compact torso, proportionate arms, defined but connected waist, full hips, upper-thigh mass, and balanced side/rear geometry.

### Garment

The white neckline must retain its broad, low, naturally irregular fold. The black body must remain a restrained long column with realistic tension and no invented construction.

### Atelier and brand

Exact accepted room. Standalone canonical icon only. No `justurban`, `wears`, `BY LULU`, `JUW`, or approximate wall mark.

### Format

One clean full-resolution full-body image. No labels, triptychs, footers, measurements, cards, or cropped panels.
